HOW LEARNING IS CHECKED & REWARDED

Study. Practice. Evaluate. Perform. Achieve.

Parents can clearly understand how children move from daily learning to regular evaluation, practical skill-building and achievement. The aim is not only to finish lessons, but to help children understand, practise and confidently use what they learn.

📅
Weekly Evaluation Short weekly checks review important topics learned during the week and help identify areas where the child may need more practice. WEEKLY PROGRESS CHECK
📝
Monthly Exam Monthly evaluations measure broader understanding, memory and progress so learning remains structured across the full 90-day journey. MONTHLY EVALUATION
🛠️
Guided / Live Practical Practical activities help children apply digital skills in real tasks such as identifying computer parts, connecting devices, typing, troubleshooting and completing technical challenges. REAL SKILL PRACTICE
🏆
Digital Achievement Eligible learning milestones and completed practical achievements can unlock digital certificates and recognition that motivate children to keep improving. MOTIVATION & REWARD
👨‍👩‍👧
What This Means for Parents & Guardians

The child follows a clear learning path instead of random screen time: learn a skill → practise it → complete weekly and monthly evaluations → use the skill in practical activities → build confidence and visible progress. This makes it easier for guardians to understand what the child is learning, what has been completed and what should be practised next.

🎓
Digital Certificates Help Keep Children Motivated Eligible achievements and completed practical milestones can be recognised with digital certificates, giving young learners a clear goal and something positive to proudly share with their family.
